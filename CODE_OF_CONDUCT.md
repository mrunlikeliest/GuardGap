# Code of Conduct

GuardGap is built in public by people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Participation should make the project more useful, safe, and
welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private architecture data, or
  vulnerability details.
- Help newcomers understand the project's evidence model and contribution process.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in any
project space.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other
contributions that violate these expectations. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security-reporting channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect
reporter privacy where possible, and respond in proportion to the behavior.
